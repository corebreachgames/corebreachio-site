Privacy policy.

Effective November 18, 2026

Core Breach does not collect personal information through its website or games, including Runefall.

Information collection and use

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information through our website or games. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies to track you.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes as part of providing its hosting service. This is separate from Core Breach’s own practices. See GitHub’s hosting documentation and GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

Contacting us

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that information only to respond to your message and provide support.

For privacy questions, contact support@corebreach.io.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
